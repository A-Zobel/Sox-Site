Bad Moon Rising

This is the Script for Bad Moon Rising, a more advanced script used for experienced players.

Townsfolk

Townsfolk are the town's good characters, working together to uncover slay the Demons.


  • Grandmother
  • Sailor
  • Chambermaid
  • Innkeeper
  • Gambler
  • Exorcist
  • Gossip
  • Courtier
  • Minstrel
  • Tea Lady
  • Fool
  • Pacifist
  • Professor

Outsiders

Outsiders are neutral or less-trusted characters who still win with the town. Their strange abilities can be helpful, but they often create suspicion.


  • Goon
  • Lunatic
  • Tinker
  • Moonchild

Minions

Minions are evil team members who support the Demon. They help deceive the town and manipulate outcomes without winning alone.


  • Bodfather
  • Devil's Advocate
  • Assassin
  • Mastermind

Demons

Demons are the evil leaders of the game. They drive the dark agenda with powerful effects and must survive to win.


  • Pukka
  • Shabaloth
  • Po
  • Zombuul
